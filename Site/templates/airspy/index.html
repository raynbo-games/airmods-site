Airspy - An Airship Monitor

Airspy is a program made to track the performance of games and organizations on the Airship platform. Click the search bar to query games and organizations by name or use "org:" or "game:" to see all entries of that type. Game pages show stats for each game and the organizations show the stats for all games owned by them in combination.
